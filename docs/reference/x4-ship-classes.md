# Reference: X4 ship classes

Copied from the vault on 2026-09-22. This describes X4: Foundations, not this
game. It is here because §2 of `../DESIGN.md` takes its system-graph model from
X4, and because the subclass-and-role split below is the catalogue that future
ship design draws on. Nothing in it has been adopted as a rule.

---

X4: Foundations ship types, base game + all DLC (Split Vendetta, Cradle of Humanity, Tides of Avarice, Kingdom End), across all major factions. Grouped by hull size (S/M/L/XL), combat and civilian subclasses both included. "Vanguard/Sentinel/E" suffixes on a hull name are political-faction production variants of the same ship, not separate designs, so they're grouped under one entry per race below.

## S — Small

| Subclass | Used for | Strengths | Weaknesses | Hull names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scout | Recon, exploration, sector mapping | Highest speed/travel-speed and sensor range in class, cheap | No or 1 weak weapon, dies to almost anything | Argon/Antigone: **Discoverer** · Teladi: **Kestrel**, **Guillemot** · Paranid/HOP: **Pegasus** · Split: **Jaguar** · Terran: **Nimcha** · Boron: **Piranha** · Xenon: **T** |
| Fighter | Dogfighting, anti-fighter escort, cheap swarms | Cheap, maneuverable, effective in numbers | Weak shields/hull, loses 1v1 to heavy fighters | Argon/Antigone: **Nova** · Teladi: **Buzzard**, **Falcon** · Paranid/HOP: **Perseus**, **Theseus** · Split: **Mamba**, **Asp** · Terran: **Takoba** · Boron: **Mako** · Xenon: **N** |
| Heavy Fighter | Anti-fighter/anti-corvette, bomber escort | More guns and shielding than a light fighter, still fast | Costlier, fewer per credit than light fighters | Argon/Antigone: **Pulsar**, **Quasar**, **Eclipse** · Paranid/HOP: **Ares** · Split: **Chimera**, **Balaur** · Terran: **Gladius**, **Kalis** · Boron: **Barracuda** · Xenon: **M** (legacy: **F**) |
| Courier (civilian) | Fast freight/personnel runs, trading | High cargo-to-speed ratio for an S ship | Unarmed or nearly so | Argon: **Callisto**, **Courier**, **Raleigh** · Teladi: **Magpie** · Terran: **Frog** · Boron: **Terrapin** |
| Miner (civilian) | Solo small-scale ore/gas mining, feeds a station or your own cargo directly | Cheap, fast to deploy, no crew commitment | Tiny yield vs. an L miner, unarmed | Argon: **Courier (Mineral)** · Teladi: **Magpie (Mineral)** · Split: **Tuatara (Mineral)** |

## M — Medium

| Subclass | Used for | Strengths | Weaknesses | Hull names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corvette | Hit-and-run strikes, anti-fighter/anti-frigate | Good speed-firepower balance | Still fragile vs. destroyer flak | Paranid/HOP: **Nemesis** · Split: **Dragon**, **Dragon Raider** · Terran: **Katana** · Boron: **Hydra**, **Hydra Regal**. Argon and Teladi have none. |
| Frigate | Multirole escort/patrol, anti-fighter turrets + forward guns | Tougher than a corvette, good turret coverage | Slower, still dies to a destroyer's main guns | Argon/Antigone: **Cerberus** · Teladi: **Osprey** · Paranid/HOP: **Gorgon** · Split: **Cobra** · Terran: **Falx** · Xenon: **P** (disputed classification). Boron has none, uses Gunboat instead. |
| Gunboat | Direct-fire support, fills the frigate niche where one's missing | Heavier forward firepower in some builds | Narrow role, only 2 factions field one | Teladi: **Peregrine** · Boron: **Thresher** |
| Transporter (civilian) | Mid-size freight, especially container/ware hauling between stations | Good cargo hold for the price, unlocked early | Unarmed | Argon: **Ides**, **Hermes**, **Demeter**, **Astrid** (unique) · Teladi: **Vulture** · Boron: **Dolphin** |
| Miner (civilian) | Mid-scale ore/gas mining | Bigger yield than an S miner, still nimble | Unarmed, smaller haul than an L miner | Terran: **Bolo** · Boron: **Porpoise** |
| Tug (civilian) | Tows salvage/wrecks and scrap cubes to processors | Cheap, purpose-built for logistics chains | No independent combat or mining use | Multi-faction (Argon-built, used widely incl. Riptide Rakers): **Manticore** |

## L — Large

| Subclass | Used for | Strengths | Weaknesses | Hull names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Destroyer | Fleet backbone, anti-capital, carries a small fighter/drone hangar | Heavy armor/shields, big main gun + turret ring | Slow, expensive, needs fighter cover vs. swarms | Argon/Antigone: **Behemoth** · Teladi: **Phoenix** · Paranid/HOP: **Odysseus** · Split: **Rattlesnake** · Terran: **Osaka**, **Sapporo** · Boron: **Ray** · Xenon: **K** |
| Freighter (civilian) | Bulk ware hauling between stations, the backbone of a trade empire | Large cargo bay, cheap per unit of cargo | Unarmed or token defense, slow | Argon: **Incarcatura** · Teladi: **Heron**, **Pelican** · Split: **Buffalo** · Terran: **Okinawa** |
| Miner (civilian) | Large-scale ore/gas mining to feed factories | High yield, big cargo hold | Unarmed, slow, expensive | Argon: **Magnetar**, **Crane E** · Teladi: **Crane** · Paranid/HOP: **Chthonios** · Split: **Wyvern**, **Alligator** · Terran: **Hokkaido** · Boron: **Rorqual** |
| Compactor (civilian) | Breaks down large wrecks into scrap cubes for tugs to haul to recyclers | Purpose-built for the recycling economy, unique niche | Only one faction produces it, no combat use | Riptide Rakers / Vigour Syndicate (Tides of Avarice DLC): **Teuta** |

## XL — Extra Large

| Subclass | Used for | Strengths | Weaknesses | Hull names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carrier | Mobile fighter/drone deployment, fleet anchor | Massive hangar capacity, resupplies its own wing | Weak offense per ton, slow, high-value target | Argon/Antigone: **Colossus** · Teladi: **Condor** · Paranid/HOP: **Zeus** · Split: **Raptor** · Terran: **Tokyo** · Boron: **Shark** (also **Guppy**, L-class light carrier). Xenon has none. |
| Battleship | Anti-capital supremacy, biggest single hull in the game | Overwhelming firepower/armor, can solo most threats | Extremely expensive/rare, very slow, huge target | Argon: **Erlking** (unique, VIG faction) · Terran: **Asgard** · Xenon: **I**. Teladi, Paranid, Split, Boron have none. |
| Resupplier / Auxiliary (civilian, fleet-support) | Mobile repair/rearm/refuel for a fleet in the field | Keeps a fleet operational indefinitely, huge cargo | Unarmed/lightly armed, must be escorted | Argon: **Nomad** · Terran: **Honshu** · Boron: **Orca** · Split: **Monitor** |
| Builder (civilian) | Constructs stations on-site from delivered build resources | Only ships that can build/expand stations at all | Extremely slow, no combat capability, very expensive | Argon: **Mammoth** · Teladi: **Albatross** · Split: **Elephant** · Terran: **Kyushu** · Boron: **Walrus** |

Xenon fields almost no civilian hulls — the faction has no economy, so no couriers/miners/freighters/builders of its own.
